# 24. SHORTS STRATEGY AND PRODUCTION

## 24.1 Topic and Audience

Evaluate:

- Broad audience potential;
- Audience relevance;
- Concept clarity;
- Shareability;
- Standalone value;
- Relationship to relevant long-form content.

## 24.2 Runtime and Structure

Preserve 20–30 seconds as one possible test format, not a universal requirement. Use the length needed to communicate the idea and deliver its payoff.

## 24.3 Hook and Loop

Start without an unnecessary introduction. Connect the end to the beginning only when a loop improves the experience. A loop is not required for every Short.

## 24.4 Trending Audio

Consider Add Sound and trending audio when relevant and properly licensed. Lowering the volume of a trending track does not guarantee that the Short will appear on that track's audio page or receive more distribution.

## 24.5 Shorts Thumbnail / Cover

Review the option to select a compelling frame or cover. The exact availability and interface may change by device and YouTube app version; check current instructions before giving click-by-click directions.

## 24.6 Audience Mismatch

Unrelated Shorts may attract viewers who are not interested in the channel's long-form videos. Investigate this using actual New, Casual, Regular, and Returning Viewer data and video performance. Do not conclude that Shorts automatically damage long-form CTR without supporting channel evidence.

## 24.7 Six Commonly Claimed Shorts Mistakes

Preserve these as risks or hypotheses, not as proven causes of a frozen channel:

1. Constantly changing the topic or format;
2. Artificially watching your own videos through alternate accounts;
3. Hastily deleting low-view videos;
4. Re-uploading the same video without a meaningful revision;
5. Publishing large volumes of low-quality videos, such as five per day;
6. Chasing unrelated trends that attract the wrong audience.

## 24.8 Prohibited or High-Risk Tactics

Do not ask a coordinated group of friends to search for, click, or watch a video to create artificial early traffic. Do not buy or manufacture views, likes, comments, subscribers, or watch time. Authentic, voluntary sharing with genuinely interested viewers is different from metric manipulation and is allowed when it is not deceptive or coerced.

Ordinary self-checking for quality control is not the same as artificial view manipulation. Do not use repeated or coordinated self-views to inflate metrics.
