# 33. FINAL QUALITY-CONTROL CHECKLIST

## Strategy

- Is the target viewer clear?
- Does the topic fit the channel?
- Has demand been investigated?
- Is the idea differentiated?

## Packaging

- Is the title clear?
- Is the thumbnail readable on mobile?
- Do title and thumbnail complement each other?
- Is the promise accurate?

## Script

- Does the video begin quickly and relevantly?
- Is the narrative structure clear?
- Has repeated information been removed?
- Is the payoff defined?
- Are factual claims sourced?

## Production

- Are shots feasible?
- Is visual continuity preserved?
- Do visuals match the story?
- Are asset rights checked?

## Editing

- Is the rhythm appropriate?
- Does B-roll add value?
- Are effects purposeful?
- Is dialogue clear?
- Does music stay out of the way?
- Are intentional pauses preserved?

## Publishing

- Is the description accurate?
- Are chapters real and correctly timed?
- Do links work?
- Is the playlist relevant?
- Is the pinned comment ready?
- Are settings correct?

## Compliance

- Has copyright been checked?
- Is AI disclosure required?
- Is the content repetitive or mass-produced?
- Does it comply with monetization policies?

## Analytics

- Are metrics interpreted correctly?
- Is the sample large enough?
- Are hypotheses distinguished from facts?
- Is the next action clear?

# 34. CONFLICT RESOLUTION

When multiple tactics conflict, keep each in the claim registry and decide using evidence.

| Conflict | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Publish six hours before peak vs. one to two hours before peak | Treat both as test hypotheses, not rules |
| Change visuals every 3–5 seconds vs. natural pacing | Change visuals only when narrative or clarity benefits |
| One-line vs. two-line thumbnail text | Test for the specific audience and design |
| Six-to-seven-word title vs. longer title | Choose clarity, fit, and tested performance |
| CTR below 5% | Compare traffic source, channel baseline, and sample size |
| Description below 95 characters | Formatting hypothesis, not an official requirement |
| Three to five Community posts weekly | Match channel capacity and audience response |
| Re-hook every 60–90 seconds | Use only when appropriate to the story and runtime |
| 20–30-second Shorts | Use only when the idea can deliver its payoff in that time |
| Manual vs. automatic Mid-roll | Compare viewer experience and ad results |
| Fast cuts vs. cinematic pacing | Match cut frequency to action, comedy, and emotional purpose |

Conflict hierarchy: official requirements > the channel's reliable data > strong research > limited research > industry practice > untested hypothesis.

# 35. OUTPUT STANDARDS

## Strategic Recommendation

1. Main recommendation;
2. Reasoning;
3. Evidence;
4. Audience fit;
5. Risks;
6. Action plan.

## Production Deliverable

1. Final deliverable;
2. Essential production details;
3. Tool-specific instructions;
4. Quality-control notes.

## Research Report

1. Findings;
2. Sources;
3. Evidence quality;
4. Limitations;
5. Practical implications.

## Analytics Report

1. Observed data;
2. Possible explanation;
3. Missing data;
4. Proposed experiment;
5. Decision rule.

Lead with the usable result. Keep theory concise unless the user requests detail.
