This registry preserves the rules, numeric claims, tactics, and research statements supplied by the user. Inclusion does not mean verification. Check the status before applying any item.

## 36.15 Cinematic Editing Claims and Technical Rules

| ID | Supplied rule or detail | Default operating instruction |
|---|---|---|
| CE-01 | “Cinematic and funny” must be defined beyond edit-service choices. | Define content type, narrative, humor, audience, references, and delivery before editing. |
| CE-02 | Cinematic does not mean dark grade, letterbox bars, or slow motion. | Prioritize story, shot choice, rhythm, sound, and coherent color. |
| CE-03 | Start with a strong moment or question in the first 5–15 seconds. | Editorial target; adapt to the footage and genre. |
| CE-04 | Establish the situation and objective quickly. | Required for clarity unless the narrative intentionally delays context. |
| CE-05 | Use progression, events, challenges, or twists. | Build a real narrative arc from the source material. |
| CE-06 | End with a climax/payoff and satisfying resolution. | Preserve the viewer's expectation and finish the story. |
| CE-07 | Avoid long logo intros. | Do not delay the content without a strategic reason. |
| CE-08 | Remove waiting, loading, repetition, and irrelevant footage. | Remove only when it does not provide context, suspense, or comedy. |
| CE-09 | Preserve pauses needed for humor or suspense. | Mandatory editorial consideration; do not remove all silence. |
| CE-10 | U.S. English-speaking target with international clarity. | Use natural American English and avoid forced slang. |
| CE-11 | Distinguish dialogue, narration, subtitles, and dubbing. | Ask or state assumptions; do not confuse these deliverables. |
| CE-12 | Long-form editing and Shorts are separate tasks. | Do not produce Shorts unless requested; export them separately. |
| CE-13 | Specify exact target duration inside the 5–20-minute range. | Use a specific target; 8–12 minutes is an example, not a rule. |
| CE-14 | Use 16:9 long-form delivery. | Standard long-form default; follow project requirements. |
| CE-15 | Match resolution and frame rate to source. | Avoid unnecessary upscaling or conversion. |
| CE-16 | Use licensed music and sound effects. | Record source and rights/terms. |
| CE-17 | Specify subtitle type. | Full burned-in, separate SRT, selective text, bilingual, or none. |
| CE-18 | Prioritize dialogue over music. | Maintain intelligibility and avoid masking. |
| CE-19 | Correct color before creative grading. | Correct exposure/white balance and match shots before applying a look. |
| CE-20 | Preserve shadows, highlights, and natural skin. | Avoid crushed blacks, clipped whites, and artificial skin. |
| CE-21 | Denoise, stabilize, and sharpen only as needed. | Avoid artifacts and over-processing. |
| CE-22 | Clipped audio cannot always be restored. | State limits; do not promise full restoration. |
| CE-23 | -14 LUFS integrated and -1 dBTP. | Starting references for testing, not official YouTube requirements. |
| CE-24 | Prepare a structure proposal before final edit. | Provide key moments, planned removals, humor direction, and runtime. |
| CE-25 | Do not guess timecodes when files are inaccessible. | Mandatory. |
| CE-26 | Check sync, subtitles, black frames, music cuts, and export. | Mandatory QC when the actual file is available. |
