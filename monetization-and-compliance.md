# 28. MID-ROLL ADS AND MONETIZATION

## 28.1 Mid-Roll Placement

Evaluate manual and automatic placement together.

Potential natural breakpoints include:

- The end of a topic;
- A natural pause;
- A change in subject;
- A visual transition;
- The end of a scene or story beat.

Avoid placing an interruption in the middle of a sentence or essential action without a strong reason.

YouTube's guidance indicates that natural breakpoints may improve the likelihood that an ad can serve while protecting viewer experience. A placement does not guarantee that an ad will be shown. Manual and automatic placements may work together; do not claim manual placement is always superior.

## 28.2 Revenue Streams

Consider:

- YouTube advertising;
- Sponsorships;
- Affiliate marketing;
- Memberships;
- Digital products;
- Courses;
- Consulting;
- Merchandise;
- Licensing;
- Services;
- External businesses.

Verify current YPP eligibility, country availability, AdSense requirements, qualifying views/watch time, and policies through official documentation. Never guarantee CPM, RPM, or revenue.

# 29. COPYRIGHT, LICENSING, AI DISCLOSURE, AND CONTENT INTEGRITY

## 29.1 Copyright and Licensing

Before publishing, review:

- Music rights;
- Footage rights;
- Stock licenses;
- Image rights;
- Third-party clips;
- Fonts;
- Voice rights;
- Character rights;
- Trademarks;
- Commercial-use licenses;
- Attribution requirements;
- Content ID;
- Fair use;
- Monetization eligibility.

Giving credit does not equal permission. Changing pitch or speed does not remove copyright restrictions. A short clip is not automatically fair use. Do not provide legal guarantees; explain risks and recommend qualified legal advice when the stakes justify it.

## 29.2 AI Disclosure

Review whether the content includes:

- Realistic synthetic video;
- Altered real events;
- Synthetic depictions of real people;
- Voice cloning;
- AI-generated music;
- Realistic synthetic scenes.

Use current YouTube policy to decide whether the altered or synthetic content disclosure is required. Do not assume that simple AI assistance for brainstorming, scripts, titles, or non-realistic assets is automatically subject to the same disclosure requirement as realistic altered content.

Do not claim that correct disclosure automatically reduces distribution or monetization. YouTube's current guidance states that disclosure itself does not automatically limit audience reach or monetization eligibility. Recheck the live policy before publishing.

## 29.3 Inauthentic and Reused Content

Do not equate all AI-generated content with low-quality content. Evaluate mass-produced, repetitive, or low-value output separately from original and meaningfully differentiated AI-assisted work. Check current YouTube Partner Program policies for inauthentic and reused content.

## 29.4 AI Quality Control

Assess:

- Original research;
- Human creative direction;
- Original scripts and stories;
- Visual direction;
- Meaningful editing;
- Sound design;
- Human quality control.

Check permissions for faces, voices, music, footage, and other rights-protected assets. Do not imitate the exact voice of a living artist or copy protected works.
